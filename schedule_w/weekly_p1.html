➊ Experimental Inventory

In the first project, you will select one list from the options provided below and create a publication based on it. The key to this project is how you interpret the list and how you translate that interpretation into design. The form of your publication is completely open. Depending on your interpretation, your publication can be highly experimental, classical, or take any other approach that you find appropriate for the content.


➋ Narrative Composition

This project explores how typography and imagery can shape the overall mood and tone of a book. A series of workshops will guide you through carefully typesetting long-form text and developing your own visual themes that respond to the text you choose. For your source text, you may select any work from the Plays/Films/Dramas category on Project Gutenberg.
